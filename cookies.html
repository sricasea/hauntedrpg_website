Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on hauntedrpg.com, operated by Burzerkerr Studios LLC. It works alongside our Privacy Policy. We keep this simple: nothing used for advertising or analytics loads until you choose "Accept" on our cookie banner.

What we use

_fbp, fr and related Meta (Facebook) pixel cookies Measure our ad performance and help us reach people likely to enjoy Haunted. Only after you select "Accept" Up to ~90 days (set by Meta)

The Meta pixel

The Meta pixel is a small piece of code that sets cookies and reports activity to Meta Platforms. It does not load, and no advertising cookies are set, unless you select "Accept" on the banner. If you select "Decline," the pixel never loads. Some pixel data may also be sent to Meta server-to-server via Meta's Conversions API, again only with your consent.

Changing your choice

You can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie choices" link in the footer of our home page, which clears your stored preference and shows the banner again. You can also delete cookies and site data directly in your browser settings.

Do Not Track and global signals

Because our advertising cookies are off by default and load only on explicit consent, declining the banner has the same effect as opting out. We may honor recognized browser privacy signals where required by law.
